Hibachi Chicken

🥗
CategoryLunch
Time45 min
Difficultyeasy

Ingredients

  • hibachi chicken sauce:
  • 1 1/2 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • sesame seeds (optional)
  • hibachi chicken:
  • cube chicken breasts
  • coat in hibachi sauce
  • cook in light olive oil until golden brown on medium/high heat
  • fried rice:
  • heat a cast iron pan on medium heat with olive oil
  • sauté 1/4 a diced white onion until translucent
  • stir 3 minced garlic cloves (add butter optional)
  • mix in 1-2 cups mixed vegetables until coated with garlic & onion
  • make a hole in center of pan and scramble 1 egg
  • next add in 1-2 cups cooked white rice and 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Yum yum sauce:
  • 3 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar (replacement vinegar + pinch of sugar)
  • 1 tsp teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tsp sriracha (replacement honey chili flakes)
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
